The PowerPort Health Risk Connection

If you or a loved one had a Bard PowerPort or AngioDynamics implanted port catheter, you could be entitled to significant compensation.

  • The Bard PowerPort is an implanted vascular access device used during chemotherapy, blood transfusions, and long-term IV treatments.
  • Lawsuits allege the device's catheter material can fracture, migrate, or degrade — releasing fragments into the bloodstream and causing severe internal injuries.
  • Bard and AngioDynamics allegedly failed to warn patients and physicians about the rate of fracture, migration, infection, and thrombosis tied to these devices.
  • Cases have been consolidated in MDL 3081, with bellwether trials beginning March 2026 — meaning time is limited to file a claim.

Qualifying Injuries

If The Port Caused Any Of These, You May Qualify

The Bard PowerPort and similar implanted port catheters have been linked to several serious injury patterns. Each carries a different settlement value range.

Catheter Fracture

Device tubing breaks or degrades, releasing fragments into the bloodstream. May cause vascular damage or embolism.

Up to $350K

Migration

Catheter moves from intended position into heart, lungs, or other organs. Often requires emergency removal.

Up to $350K

Infection

Bacteria enter bloodstream through device defects. May lead to sepsis, requiring hospitalisation and removal.

$30K–$100K

Thrombosis

Blood clot forms around the device. May travel to lungs (pulmonary embolism) or cause deep vein thrombosis.

$100K–$250K

Who Qualifies For A Claim?

If you or a loved one had an implanted chest port catheter and experienced any of the following, you may be entitled to compensation.

  • You had a Bard PowerPort or AngioDynamics port implanted in your chest
  • The port was used for chemotherapy, Crohn's, sickle cell, RA, transfusions, or parenteral nutrition
  • The device caused infection, fracture, migration, or organ damage
  • The port was removed, or removal is scheduled
  • Injury was discovered within the last 5 years (statute of limitations)
  • You are not currently represented by another firm for this matter

Why This Matters

What Bard Knew, And When

Bard Access Systems and AngioDynamics manufactured implanted port catheters used by hundreds of thousands of patients undergoing chemotherapy, long-term IV treatments, and other prolonged vascular access needs.

Plaintiffs in MDL 3081 allege that Bard knew its PowerPort devices had a higher rate of fracture and migration than competing products, but failed to warn patients or physicians of the risk. Internal documents may show the company was aware of deteriorating catheter material as early as the mid-2010s.

When the device fractures, fragments can travel through the bloodstream, perforate blood vessels, lodge in the heart, or cause life-threatening pulmonary embolisms. One study found 50% of patients with PowerPort complications required catheter removal due to infection.

If you or a loved one was harmed by a Bard PowerPort, you have a legal right to seek compensation. There is no fee to investigate your case.
